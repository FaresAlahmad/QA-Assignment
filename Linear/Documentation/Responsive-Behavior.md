# Responsive Behavior

## Overview

Responsive behavior was considered during web testing to verify that important Amazon functionality remained usable across different browser window sizes.

The focus was on whether important pages, fields, navigation elements, and user actions remained accessible and usable when the available screen space changed.

## Areas Tested

Responsive behavior was considered across the following areas:

* Registration and sign-in
* OTP verification
* Product navigation
* Product information
* Cart
* Checkout
* Address entry

## Registration and Sign-in

The registration and sign-in pages were checked to ensure that the main input fields and authentication controls remained accessible and usable.

No major responsive-layout issue was identified during the testing performed.

## OTP Verification

The OTP verification page was checked as part of the authentication flow.

The missing OTP field observed during testing was documented separately as an OTP functionality/usability issue. It was not classified as a responsive-layout defect because refreshing the page caused the field to appear.

## Product Navigation

Product navigation and product information were checked to ensure that important content remained accessible within the available browser window.

No major responsive-layout issue was identified during the testing performed.

## Cart and Checkout

The cart and checkout flow were checked to ensure that users could access the relevant controls and information.

The testing included address entry and navigation through the checkout process.

No major responsive-layout issue was identified during the testing performed.

## Result

No major responsive-layout defect was identified during the testing performed.

## Limitations

Responsive testing was limited to the screen sizes and browser environments available during the testing sessions. It did not cover every possible desktop, tablet, or mobile viewport size.

A more comprehensive responsive test would include predefined viewport sizes and additional devices and browsers.
